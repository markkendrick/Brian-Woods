# Southern California residential development — knowledge summary

Cleaned from the September 11, 2026 research report. This is a landowner-marketing and opportunity-screening brief, not a finding that specific land is ready to build.

## What Brian is looking for

- First search: western Riverside County for 100–200-home subdivisions.
- Separate apartment search: Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Hemet, and Ontario.
- Recommended client: an owner or authorized decision-maker with enough residential land, an approved or plausible path, and a need for feasibility, approvals, infrastructure, or project management. Include unfinished communities and phases inside larger plans.
- A 100-unit apartment building is not the same product as 100 separately saleable home lots.
- Two-story apartment limits change land need and can make a site uneconomic.

## First-round areas

| Area | Fit | First issue to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menifee | First-round subdivisions; phases in larger plans | Entitlement, access, drainage, off-sites |
| Perris | First-round ~100–200 detached homes | Airport compatibility, surrounding uses, tract conditions |
| Hemet–San Jacinto | First-round subdivisions; selected apartments | Price point, utilities, map and phasing |
| Beaumont–Banning | First-round subdivision and phased communities | Infrastructure obligations, district charges, builder competition |
| Wildomar | First-round apartments; selective subdivisions | Lease-up competition, site layout, service costs |
| Lake Elsinore | First-round low-rise apartments; selective subdivisions | Drainage, access, terrain, zoning |
| Ontario / Ontario Ranch | Parallel apartments or a defined tract phase | Land control, specific plan, fees |
| Winchester / French Valley | Conditional: existing approved residential sites only | Actual jurisdiction and adopted rights |

Do not treat the Inland Empire as one market. Test product, price or rent, competition, and improvement burden around the actual parcel.

## Secondary screens

Hesperia / Victor Valley, Indio / Coachella Valley, and Palmdale / Antelope Valley are second-round. San Diego, Ventura, and coastal LA–OC are selective, favoring proven rights or infill.

These show scale and process. They are not properties Brian is offering.

## Screening arithmetic (illustrative only)

Conventional 100–200-home first-pass window: about 20–70 gross acres. Two-story apartments: start around 5–15 acres, then have an architect test layout. Gross acreage is not a promised lot count.
